BillMitra — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

BillMitra ("the app") is a GST billing app for small businesses.

Data we collect

The app does not collect, upload or share any personal or business data. Everything you enter — business details, parties, items, invoices and payments — is stored only on your own device (browser storage). We have no server that receives this data.

Backups

When you choose "Backup", a file is created on your device. Where you keep or share that file (e.g. Google Drive, WhatsApp) is your choice.

WhatsApp, printing and UPI

When you tap WhatsApp, the app opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message; you decide whether to send it. UPI QR codes are generated on the device.

Children

The app is meant for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version adds cloud sync, this policy will be updated before that feature is enabled.
